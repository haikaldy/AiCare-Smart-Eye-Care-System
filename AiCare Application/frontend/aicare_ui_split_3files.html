AiCare Tips

Quick tips

👀

What is 20-20-20 rule?

After 20 minutes of screen use, look away for 20 seconds. AiCare reminds you and resets the session timer after the break is completed.

🔔

Notify User

AiCare shows Windows notification banners with sound when the user is too close, too far, returns to a safe distance, or completes a break.

%

What does confidence mean?

Confidence shows how sure YOLO26s is about the detected label. It is not the exact screen distance, but a model certainty score.

🎥

How does this app work?

The webcam captures frames, Flask sends them to the YOLO model, and AiCare classifies the viewing distance as Too Close, Safe, or Too Far.
